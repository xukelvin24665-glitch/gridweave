HACKSHSID 2026 · TRACK 2
01

Protect service

Keep online inference in its original hour. Finish every reserved job within its window, without exceeding GPU or grid capacity.

02

Schedule together

Minimize energy cost, battery wear, peak fees, and a chosen carbon value. Charging and discharging cannot happen together.

03

Replan each hour

Observe current supply, solve again, and conservatively assume no future renewables in the final four hours. Restore the battery’s initial energy by day’s end.

Where this model stops

Hourly intervals and fluid GPU equivalents do not model millisecond latency, GPU topology, checkpoint overhead, or thermal transients. All job reservations and inference demand are known. The closing-hours guard reduces terminal risk but cannot guarantee feasibility under arbitrary forecast errors. Renewable, battery, and server capital costs are excluded. Grid intensity is an accounting signal, not a causal emissions estimate. Synthetic experiments demonstrate model behavior; deployment needs real traces and validation.
